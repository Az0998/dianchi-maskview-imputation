Dianchi · Water Quality · Mask-View

Mask-View Spatiotemporal Imputation for Block-Missing Automatic Monitoring Networks

An open benchmark and method for reconstructing multi-station water-quality series under operational missingness (sensor downtime, station outages) in the Dianchi Lake basin. Target journal: Water (MDPI).

GitHub Repository Open Dataset Manuscript (docx) Project Site

105benchmark settings (7×5×3)

Why Mask-View

Automatic stations fail as blocks, not only as scattered MCAR holes. Mask-View trains on complementary failure patterns (point, block_time, sensor, station, mixed) with reconstruction and (optional) cross-view consistency, on a MixHop spatiotemporal graph.
