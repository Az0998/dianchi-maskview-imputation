Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript
“Mask-View Spatiotemporal Imputation for Block-Missing Water-Quality Monitoring Networks: A Dianchi Lake Basin Benchmark”
for publication in Water.

The manuscript addresses a practical gap in water-quality informatics: automatic monitoring series are dominated by block-structured missingness, yet many ML papers evaluate only MCAR holes. We propose Mask-View augmentation and a MixHop spatiotemporal imputer, and we provide a reproducible open benchmark on the Dianchi 22-station dataset with classical and deep baselines, ablations and DO-focused metrics. We believe the work fits the Water Quality / Hydroinformatics scope and meets MDPI expectations for open data and reproducible methods.
